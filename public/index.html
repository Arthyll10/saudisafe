About notifications →

BUILT FOR CONTEXT

Stay informed. Read the source.

SaudiSafe filters public reporting from selected publishers. Coverage may be incomplete, and automated matching can miss context. Precise incident locations are not inferred from headlines.

How notifications work

Reports published within two hours can be sent to the operator’s configured Telegram chat. The first run establishes a baseline; duplicate URLs and identical headlines are suppressed. Ask the operator for channel access.

Scheduled monitoring targets 15-minute intervals when enabled. Local collection runs when the operator starts it; timing is never guaranteed.
