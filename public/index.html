Reading the labels

Developing report
Reported by a publisher with a known publication time. Not independently corroborated.
Unverified
Found through discovery search with an unknown publication time. Never sent as an alert.
Why are there no “confirmed” or “official” labels?

Those labels need authenticated safety announcements or strong corroboration. SaudiSafe never assigns them automatically, and the same story at several outlets is not treated as confirmation.

How SaudiSafe works

Collection

An automated job reads eight publisher news feeds and a discovery search about every 15 minutes. Opening this page never starts a collection; it shows what was last saved.

Filtering

A headline is kept only when it names a Saudi location and a security or emergency term, in English or Arabic. Opinion, analysis and anniversary pieces are left out. Matching is by keyword, so some reports are missed.

Telegram alerts

New publisher reports from the last two hours can be sent to the operator’s Telegram channel, each at most once. Ask the operator for channel access.

Locations

A city named in a headline does not prove where an event happened, so exact incident locations are never shown. No maps or tracking are produced.
